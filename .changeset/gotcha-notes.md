Add notes: gotchas, workarounds and invariants as anchored records in `.poetics/notes/`. `@poetics/core` adds the `Note` types; `@poetics/store` validates notes, warns when an anchored unit has changed since a note was confirmed, and renders `GOTCHAS.md` with `renderGotchas`; `@poetics/mcp` defines the `poetics_notes` and `poetics_record_note` tools.
